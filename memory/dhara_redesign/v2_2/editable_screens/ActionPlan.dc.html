Deposit not returned Maharashtra
Your right

Your landlord must return your deposit — and can only keep money for real, proven damage.

Holding a deposit without a lawful reason is not allowed. You can demand it back in writing, and then go to the Rent Authority or consumer forum.

Rent receipts, the agreement, and photos of the empty house. Save them in My Files.

You can file a complaint with the Rent Authority or the consumer forum. No lawyer needed to start.
