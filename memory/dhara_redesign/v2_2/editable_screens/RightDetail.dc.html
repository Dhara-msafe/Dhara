Police & FIR

A police station must register your FIR for a serious crime — even if it happened elsewhere.

This is called a Zero FIR. The police cannot refuse because the crime happened in another area. They record it and send it to the right station.

When this helps you
The police tell you the crime is "not in our area".
You were travelling when something happened to you.
Comes from
BNSS, 2023 · Zero FIR provision
Supreme Court: Lalita Kumari v. State of U.P.
