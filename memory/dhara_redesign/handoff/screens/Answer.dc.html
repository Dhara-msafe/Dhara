“What is the POSH Act and who does it protect?”

In short

The POSH Act protects every woman at a workplace from sexual harassment — whether she is an employee, a visitor, a client, or works from home. It applies to any workplace with 10 or more people.

What it gives you
The right to complain to an Internal Committee (ICC) at your workplace.
A reply within 90 days, and protection from being punished for complaining.
If there is no ICC, you can go to the Local Committee in your district.
